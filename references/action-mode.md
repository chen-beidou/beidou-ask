# Action, chase, and physical-comedy mode

Use when physical objectives, attack/defense, pursuit, impact, destruction, or magic drive the scene.

## Action unit

```text
initiation → defense/evasion/contact → force direction and displacement
→ recovery choice → changed tactical state
```

Every major move needs an initiator, path, contact or miss, physical result, and new state. A cut changes the viewing point, not the number of times the move occurs.

## Capacity

- 15-second live action: use one to four major action units by density—low 5–7 shots, standard 7–10, high 10–14.
- 30-second live action: usually three to six major units and 10–18 shots with a midpoint reversal. Preserve readable contact and recovery instead of maximizing count.
- 15-second 2D/3D/fantasy: three or four major units, usually 12–18 shots.
- One-vs-many: at most one or two foreground opponents execute precise attacks simultaneously; others block routes, reposition, or threaten.
- Gunfight, boss, or spell Clip: one local objective and two or three decisive causal changes.

If capacity is exceeded, remove secondary moves or split Clips. Do not compress unrelated attacks into an unreadable chain.

## Coverage functions

Use different views of the same continuous unit:

- wide view for geography and displacement
- medium view for initiation and defense
- detail for grip, footwork, weapon contact, or trigger action
- POV for threat approach
- reaction close-up for decisive force
- recovery view for the new tactical state

Use “承接、动作未停、顺势、同时” to preserve continuity. Do not restart the move after each cut.

## Physical clarity

State direction, side, hand, contact point, force, balance, and recovery. For weapons, retain owner, hand, orientation, damage, and loss. For falls, show cause, trajectory, impact, and recovery rather than teleporting to the floor.

## Impact and safety

Prefer readable timing and material response over particle decoration. Treat blood, injury, or dangerous imitation according to the user's intended rating and platform; do not add graphic detail that the request does not require.
