# Emotion performance engine

## Performance equation

```text
believable performance = surface mask + underlying emotion + relationship objective
                       + trigger + physiological leak + control strategy
                       + speech behavior + listener response + changed relation
```

An emotion word alone is not direction. Translate it into observable cause and behavior.

## Layered emotion

Prefer a protective surface over a deeper need:

| Surface | May protect | Useful contradiction |
|---|---|---|
| anger | shame, fear, grief | hard words with retreating feet |
| indifference | longing, hurt | flat voice with persistent gaze |
| humor | fear, embarrassment | smiling mouth with failed inhale |
| politeness | hostility, panic | formal wording with white knuckles |
| control | insecurity, guilt | precise motions interrupted by one error |
| caretaking | guilt, fear of abandonment | comforting the listener while avoiding the trigger |

## Observable channels

Choose two or three channels per Clip; do not stack every symptom.

| Emotion family | Physiological leak | Speech behavior | Control strategy |
|---|---|---|---|
| anger | jaw tension, nasal exhale, rigid neck | clipped phrases, interruption, overformal address | lower volume, align objects |
| fear | shallow breath, exit scan, backward weight | fast denial, confirmation loop, false start | forced stillness, topic shift |
| shame | collapsed posture, face shielding, downward gaze | fading endings, self-mockery, indirect answer | apology, smile, self-devaluation |
| guilt | eye avoidance, swallow, hand suspension | trigger-word block, correction, overexplanation | caretaking, giving the listener an excuse |
| jealousy | fixed gaze, delayed smile, object monitoring | repeated question, irony, minimization | perform indifference |
| longing | gaze lingers then leaves, hand nears without touch | detour around the need, softened ordinary words | joke, practical small talk |
| disgust | nostril contraction, upper-lip lift, withdrawal | distant address, short answer | formal distance |
| suspicion | sudden stillness, reduced blinking, scan | echo a keyword, delayed reply, precise question | pretend acceptance |
| joy | faster breath and movement, widened gesture | overlap, repetition, unfinished sentence | suppress smile, redirect energy |
| relief | delayed exhale, released shoulders, weakened knees | ordinary word breaks, shorter complete sentence | turn away, comfort another person |

## Trigger placement

Put speech failure at the psychologically difficult word, name, pronoun, admission, or object. The mouth may form before sound; a breath may fail; a swallow may replace the word; a second attempt may succeed. Do not distribute stuttering evenly across the line.

## Control makes emotion visible

Write both the leak and the attempt to hide it:

```text
hand reaches → tremor appears → character pins the hand under the table
mouth forms the name → no sound → character replaces the name with “他”
eyes wet → character looks into a bright light → no tear falls
```

Escalate through different channels rather than repeating the same reaction.

## Listener causality

The listener must perceive or misread a leak and change behavior. A reaction shot that changes nothing is decorative. Valid changes include softening pressure, interrupting, withdrawing, moving closer, surrendering a prop, or exploiting weakness.

## Show, don't tell (three-detail check)

The model renders bodies, not feelings. Never write an emotion word alone — translate it into three physical facts. Before shipping any shot, it must carry **at least one** from each row. If a shot has a zero, fix the shot:

| Check | Must have one of |
|---|---|
| 环境压力 | 光的来源/天气/表面质感/风/温度带来的身体反应 |
| 身体微动作 | 下巴绷紧、吞咽、手指收进掌心、呼吸停一拍、重心后移、视线下移 |
| 声音锚点 | 一句具体声响：衣物摩擦、脚步、金属"咔"、吞咽、远处可命名的环境声 |

> 坏例：`她感到孤独。` → 好例：`镜头压在她视线空白一侧。她指腹刮过旧桌沿，一下，两下；窗外雨把灯牌字冲得发糊。`

## Final-frame rule

Each shot must end on a clear **destination image** — the model treats the ending as the emotional point it lands on. Write the last thing the audience sees, not just the last action. If the ending is ambiguous ("人物站着"), give it a destination ("人物背对镜头走进光里，身后灯火一盏盏灭").
