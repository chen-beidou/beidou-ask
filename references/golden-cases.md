# Golden behavioral cases

Use these as decision tests, not story templates.

For the delivery *shape* (segment header, `素材说明`, `一句话概述`, `具体情节`, per-shot execution fields, `段尾定格帧＝下一段起幅`, and `全局补充`), follow [`sample-output.md`](sample-output.md); it is the complete uncompressed format baseline and is checked by the release gate. Use the cases below for *decisions*, and never copy story content from either file.

## Case A: 15s, 9:16, jealousy protecting fear of loss

- Surface: joking indifference.
- Trigger: the other person's packed suitcase.
- First leak: delayed smile while tracking the suitcase handle.
- Speech behavior: repeats a practical question; blocks on the destination name.
- Listener response: stops packing rather than delivering another line.
- Vertical staging: one character near foreground at lower height; the suitcase central-low; the listener stands deeper near the door.
- Ending change: the suitcase handle is released, but nobody promises to stay.
- Pass condition: no shouting or generic crying; the listener's changed action proves the leak was perceived.

## Case B: 30s, 16:9, interrogation becomes physical escape

- First half is dialogue mode: interrogator controls chair, exit, and evidence bag.
- Midpoint: suspect notices an unlocked side door reflected in metal.
- Second half is action mode: diversion, chair obstruction, two-step escape attempt, interception.
- Horizontal staging: evidence and exit occupy opposite sides, forcing a visible tactical choice.
- Continuity: suspect's cuffed left wrist, evidence-bag ownership, chair position, and opened door persist.
- Ending change: suspect fails to leave but gains the evidence, reversing information power.
- Pass condition: dialogue does not continue unchanged after the escape begins; action shots continue one causal movement rather than replaying it.

## Case C: Repair a failed 30s emotional scene

- Symptom: actor stutters across every sentence and cries from the first shot.
- Repair: restore the opening mask; select two leak channels; move the speech block to one trigger name at 20–24 seconds; give the listener a tactic change; preserve all unaffected lines and positions.
- Pass condition: the second half reveals deeper emotion rather than merely increasing volume.
